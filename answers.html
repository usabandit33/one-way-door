Questions people ask after they hear scratching above the ceiling

What is a one-way door for wildlife?

It is a flap or cage that lets the animal walk out and blocks the way back. It only works if every other entry is already closed and young can follow the adult.

Should I trap a raccoon in the attic or use exclusion?

A trap that catches only the adult can leave a nest in the insulation. Ask how they inspect for young before they set steel or seal the last gap.

Can Illinois operators relocate a raccoon to another property?

Illinois does not treat urban raccoons like a free drop-off at a forest preserve. Ask what the operator is actually allowed to do on your lot — often release on the same property only.

Who repairs the hole after wildlife leaves?

A removal visit that leaves the soffit open is an invitation for the next animal. Ask who does the carpentry and whether that repair is in the written total.

Do I need attic cleanup after raccoons?

Urine, feces, and shredded batting are a second job. Ask whether sanitation and insulation replacement are included.

Will Chicago Animal Care take a raccoon out of my attic?

Chicago Animal Care is not a private attic crew. For animals in an attic you usually call a licensed operator. Chicago phone numbers are listed separately.

What license should I ask for in Illinois?

Ask for the Nuisance Wildlife Control Operator number printed on the invoice and look it up before anyone opens the fascia.
