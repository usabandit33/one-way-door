Questions people search after they hear scratching above the ceiling

What is a one-way door?

It is a flap or cage that lets the animal walk out and blocks the way back. It only works if every other entry is already closed and young can follow the adult.

Trap now or wait?

A trap that catches only the adult can leave a nest in the insulation. Ask how they inspect for young before they set steel or seal the last gap.

Can they relocate the raccoon?

Illinois does not treat urban raccoons like a free drop-off at a forest preserve. Ask what the operator is actually allowed to do on your lot.

Who seals the hole?

A removal visit that leaves the soffit open is an invitation for the next animal. Ask who does the carpentry and when.

Cleanup and insulation

Urine, feces, and shredded batting are a second job. Ask whether sanitation is in the written total.

Will the city come?

Chicago Animal Care is not a private attic crew. For animals in an attic you usually call a licensed operator. Chicago phone numbers are listed separately.

License lookup

Ask for the Nuisance Wildlife Control Operator number printed on the invoice and look it up before anyone cuts a hole in the fascia.
